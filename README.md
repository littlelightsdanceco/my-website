MEET THE FOUNDER
Kayla Berry
Kayla is a Nashville native and lifelong performer — trained as a dancer, singer, and actress, with years of experience bringing energy and warmth to every room she steps into.
She started Little Lights Dance Co to give young kids that same joy in the place they already spend their day, turning a weekly 30 minutes into something they truly look forward to.
Off the stage, Kayla is also a mom — so she knows firsthand what parents and little ones both need from a class like this: consistency, encouragement, and a whole lot of fun.
